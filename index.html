              How GlobalChat works

              GlobalChat is one public room. Anyone with the link can read and post, and there are no accounts, emails, or passwords.

              Your guest identity

              When you open the page you get a random name like QuietFox_1937 and a temporary guest session that lasts for this browser tab. A new tab means a new name. Names are random labels, not proof of who anyone is.

              Messages disappear

              Every message is hidden one hour after it's sent and deleted from the database shortly after. Messages fade slightly in their last few minutes. Anyone in the room may have read, copied, or screenshotted it in the meantime, and copies can remain for a limited time in our service providers' backups and logs.

              What other people see

              Other visitors see your random name in the "Online now" list while your tab is open, a "typing…" hint while you write, your messages, and when you sent them. They never see your IP address, location, or device.

              What we and our providers process

              GlobalChat isn't fully anonymous. To run and protect the service, our hosting and database providers process technical data such as IP addresses, request logs, and an anonymous session ID. This is used for security, rate limiting, and abuse prevention, not to show your identity to others.

              Photos and GIFs

              Images you share are visible only while their message is, and are deleted from storage shortly after it expires or is deleted. Photos are re-saved in your browser before upload, which removes hidden details such as location data. GIFs are uploaded as they are.

              Reactions and polls

              Everyone sees how many people reacted or voted, never who.

              Fun stuff

              Type /roll, /roll 20, /flip or /8ball your question: the server rolls the dice, so nobody can fake a result. Formatting: *bold*, _italic_, `code` and ||spoiler||. The room bot posts a question of the day and, while people are chatting, a trivia question every 15 minutes. First right answer wins.

              Voice notes, stickers and flags

              Voice notes (up to 30 seconds) are stored privately like photos and deleted after their message. Your country flag is optional, chosen by you in settings, and never detected from your location.

              Editing, muting, translating

              You can edit a text message for 2 minutes after sending it; edited messages are marked. Muting someone hides their messages only on your screen. Translate uses your browser's built-in translator when it has one; otherwise it opens Google Translate in a new tab, which sends that message's text to Google.

              Safety

              Common swear words are masked automatically. A message reported by 3 different people is hidden until a moderator reviews it. Moderators can also turn on slow mode or pin a message.

              Reports

              When you report a message, a copy of it is kept for moderators even after it disappears from the room, so they can review it. Reports are visible only to moderators.

              Found a bug or have feedback?

              Send us a message on Instagram: @global.chat_. To report a harmful message, use Report on the message instead, so moderators get a copy of it.

              Your settings

              Theme, colour, sounds, your flag choice, muted names and unsent drafts are stored only in this browser.
